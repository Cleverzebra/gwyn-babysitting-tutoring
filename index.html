📚 Tutoring & Academic Coaching

Elementary through high school

I've tutored lower, middle, and high school students in math and science, and I love the moment something finally clicks.

  • Math & science: homework help, concept review, and getting ahead
  • Writing: brainstorming, drafts, and revision that keeps the student's own voice
  • Organization & executive function: planners, schedules, and study systems that actually stick
  • Test prep: study plans and practice that build real confidence
  • School applications: essays, timelines, and interview practice (I'm in the middle of the college process myself, so it's fresh)
  • Between sessions: quick check-ins by email or Zoom whenever questions come up
💜 Neurodivergent kids welcome! I'm happy to work on strategies, routines, and prep that fit how each student learns best.
